Development Process

Development of G-Ramps will happen in two ways simultaneously - breadth-wise and depth-wise development.

I will work on multiple g-ramps. Breadth-wise development involve working little bit on every step of every ramp.

And depth-wise development involve working on few particular steps of a particular ramp intensely.

Current Depth-Wise Development Plan

My first priority is to focus on most difficult parts of AI algorithms. There are many difficult parts. Currently, I am focusing on one of them which is in third ramp, post “BMM” step.
